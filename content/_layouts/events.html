Events at Mixi

MIXI hosts events throughout the year that help us investigate the wide range of concerns about STEM and the imagination. Our events include the MIXI Lecture Series where we invite leading thinkers to discuss their work. Reading Groups, held online or in person, bring together scholars, activists, artists, practitioners and students meet regularly to discuss key works. Workshops help us explore new technologies, share knowledge about the latest methods, and work together on MIXI projects. Our conferences and seminars gather transdisciplinary thinkers to share their work while expanding the boundaries.
