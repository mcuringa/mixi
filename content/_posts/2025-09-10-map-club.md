We are please to announce the brand new **Maps @ MIXI** critical cartography club.
The club is a fun, informal place to talk about and make maps, share your work,
check out interesting parts of NYC on club outings, and learn new geospatial skills.

During our weekly meetings we will participate in informal workshops,
work on our own and collaborative projects, hear from guest speakers,
share resources, and head off site on to explore the city, gather data, 
and test our maps.

_We will also have snacks._

{% endmd %}
