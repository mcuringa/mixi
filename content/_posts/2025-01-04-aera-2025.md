Matt Curinga and Liz de Freitas will be at AEAR 2025 in Denver, Colorado to present research from several
MIXI projects. We are also going to host and <b class="fw-bold">Adelphi Happy Hour</b> with information about
our new [PhD in Learning Sciences](/degrees) and its funded scholarships. _Details to follow._

**Papers/Presentations**

1. **How the concept of racial entropy came to shape ideas about school segregation: A critical analysis of the cybernetic assumptions in school demographic models.** Elizabeth de Freitas, Matthew X. Curinga.
2. **Learning under algorithmic conditions: Critical perspectives on AI and curriculum.** de Freitas and Curinga, along with co-editors Ezekiel Dixon-Román and Taylor Webb will present chapters from the forthcoming University of Minnesota Press book.
3. **Mapping Student Flow Through Fragmented School Buildings.** in the session structured poster session, _Mapping the Field: The Role of Space in Educational Equity_. Elizabeth de Freitas, Matthew X. Curinga.

**More details (with time and place) once the schedule is released.**
