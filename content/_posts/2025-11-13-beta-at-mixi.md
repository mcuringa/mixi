Put your local park on the map! Discover how to leverage digital
literacy to promote healthy, community centered stewardship
of open-space data. See how we use free online tools like
[OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org) (a wiki map of the world), 
[Mapillary](https://www.mapillary.com/) (a global
map of street-level imagery), and more to spotlight local
community spaces. Presented by [BetaNYC](https://beta.nyc), the co-organizers of
[Open Data Week](https://opendataweek.nyc/), School of Data, CityCamp NYC, and more
amazing civic tech events. Come learn how to grow your
network and enhance your digital and data literacy!
