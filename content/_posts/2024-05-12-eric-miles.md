Teaching artist and muralist [Eric Miles](https://www.groundswell.nyc/people/eric-miles)
is going to co-teach the [Design Lab](/courses/design-lab) 
with Dr. Curinga this summer. Eric brings a wealth of experience as an art
educator in NYC Public Schools and as a teaching artist with
[BRIC](https://bricartsmedia.org/), [Groundswell](https://www.groundswell.nyc/),
and other NYC community organizations.

Eric and Matt are working together to design tech-infused art projects, including
a new wearable fashion project...more details soon.
