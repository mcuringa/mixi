We are <b>extremely excited</b> to announce that the 
<a href="/degrees">PhD in Learning Sciences</a> has launched
and is accepting applicants. Doc students will prepare for
academic and <a href="/research">research</a> positions, working 
closely with MIXI faculty.
</p>

Highlights of the program include:

- full funding with a stipend for highly qualified full time students
- hybrid coursework based out of Adelphi's New York City graduate campus
- opportunities to collaborate and publish with MIXI researches
