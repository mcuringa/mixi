Class Sessions

S1 Monday June 22: Maker Education

We will get to know each other a little bit and talk about the goals and themes of the design lab. We will have time to answer any questions. We will talk about the two readings and how they inform our class. Come prepared with questions, comments, and ideas for the reading discussion.

During the studio time, we will distribute the blank artists journal. You will start experimenting with the tools of the maker space as you start designing your journal.

  1. Welcome and Introductions (ice breaker poll) (30 minutes)
  2. Reading Discussion (30 minutes)
  3. Studio Time (90 minutes)
    • Eric’s table: collage and spray painting
    • Matt’s table: Cricut stencils, stickers, and portfolio sites

Key skills:

  • finding and installing fonts
  • finding suitable art online
  • scaling, cropping, and combining images
  • Cricut Design Studio
  • Cricut “Print and Cut”
  • Roland vinyl cutter
  • spray paint techniques
  • collage
  • google sites

Materials:

  • digital and physical image banks for collage
  • other collage materials (magazines, newspapers, maps, menus, wrapping paper, etc.)
  • sticker sheets
  • vinyl sheets and rolls
  • laminate sheets
  • cutting & weeding tools
  • spray paint
  • spray paint booth, craft paper, cardboard
  • glue
  • brushes

S2 Tuesday June 23: Critical Pedagogy

We are going to get a lot of work done on the collages today, with an eye for how we conceive of a living geography. We discuss one of the key works that has influenced the maker education movement, in Paulo Freire’s Pedagogy of the Oppressed. We will begin the discussion with some students reading from their journal.

We will have a more open studio than day one where we continue to cut, glue, paint, print, etc. By the end of the day everyone should have cut something with a Cricut or Roland and spray painted something.

  1. Reading Discussion (30 minutes)
  2. Studio (2½ hours)

Same materials as day 1

S3 Wednesday June 24: Art & the Public

Today we will talk about “art”, the role it plays in our society, and how it interacts with the public. We will discuss the Fishman article about Mark Bradford’s work for the Obama Presidential Center. During the studio, we will work to finish our collages, but also take some time to form groups for the final project and begin to sketch initial ideas.

  1. Reading Discussion (30 minutes)
  2. Studio (60 minutes)
  3. Collage showcase and crit
  4. Group formation and project brainstorming (30 minutes)

S4 Thursday June 25: Critical Thinking

We will discuss critical thinking in terms of Lisa Delpit’s chapter on “picking up the broom”. We will also start to talk about curriculum design and lesson planning. Before the end of the session, everyone should have a good idea of the topic and format for their “maker lesson” assignment.

During our studio time, we will begin working in teams on the final panel. Teams should consider if they want to engrave or cut anything with the laser cutter, as we will be working with this equipment during day 5.

  1. Reading Discussion (30 minutes)
  2. Introduction to Curriculum Design (30 minutes)
  3. Lesson plan exploration (60 minutes)
  4. Studio (60 minutes)

Materials:

  • various lesson plan templates
  • curriculum standards (NYS, Common Core, professional organizations, etc.)

S5 Friday June 26: Curriculum

We will continue our discussion of schools, curricula, and equity as we look at two key thinkers in the field: Nell Noddings and Gloria Ladson-Billings. We will try to incorporate their ideas into our own lesson plan designs, as we take first steps towards designing our own maker lessons.

During the studio, we will do a workshop on using the laser cutter, which can be used to engrave materials (directly on panels or on other materials like wood, acrylic, paper, etc). It can also be used to make precise cuts following vector paths (like those created in Inkscape).

  1. Reading Discussion (30 minutes)
  2. Lesson Planning (60 minutes)
  3. Laser cutting (30 minutes)
  4. Studio (60 minutes)

Materials:

  • laser cutting materials
  • laser cut samples

Key skills:

  • vector design in Inkscape
    • bezier curves
    • vector path operations (union, difference, intersection, etc.)
    • converting raster images to vectors
  • preparing raster images for engraving
    • contrast, brightness, and threshold adjustments
  • XTool
    • safety!!! (don’t cut any materials that are not approved for laser cutting)
    • measuring thickness of materials
    • selecting materials
    • choosing power and speed
    • choosing dithering options
    • engraving vs cutting
