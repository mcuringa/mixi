The Adelphi Master’s of Arts in Educational Technology is a 32-credit graduate degree offered as a fully online program, with access to facilities and faculty at the Garden City and Brooklyn campuses. Students complete 21 credits of required coursework studying cognition and learning sciences, instructional design, digital media studies, and computer science. They choose elective courses, usually in-depth looks at current themes andtechnologies, for an additional 9 credits of electives. Students complete the Master’s with a 2 credit thesis or thesis project which they design with their thesis advisor.
