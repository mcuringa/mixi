Rocket Switch Workshop

March 28, 2023 Events
#STEAM Lab #assistive technology #soldering #3D printing #makers making change #arduino

Join us in the MIXI Maker Lab to help build assistive technology switches with our partners, Makers Making Change – the community of volunteers who dedicated to building low cost, open source assistive technologies to make computing more equitable for all people with disabilities.

In this workshop, we will be building rocket switches: an alternative physical interface that allows users to navigate their computer or smart phone by pressing buttons. In this workshop we will assemble and solder 3D printed cases, electronic boards, and 3.5 mm cables.
