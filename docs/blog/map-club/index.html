Announcing Maps @ MIXI

Published September 8, 2025 News
GIS cartography mapping spatial justice responsible computing mozilla public sticky

We are please to announce the brand new Maps @ MIXI critical cartography club. The club is a fun, informal place to talk about and make maps, share your work, check out interesting parts of NYC on club outings, and learn new geospatial skills.

During our weekly meetings we will participate in informal workshops, work on our own and collaborative projects, hear from guest speakers, share resources, and head off site on to explore the city, gather data, and test our maps.

We will also have snacks.

About the Club

  • make digital maps that tell NYC stories
  • meet with professional map makers and researchers
  • learn new GIS, computing, design, and data science skills

Join Us

  • open to everyone—no experience necessary
  • weekly meetings on Thursday, 5-6:30pm, starting September 11
  • it’s free! (no fees or dues)
  • meet At Adelphi’s MIXI lab @ the St. Francis College campus in Brooklyn (179 Livingston St, 7th Floor)
  • fill out the interest form for more info and to stay in touch https://forms.gle/MscaMdZUkd4Tx26J7

Maps @ MIXI is supported by the Mozilla Foundation as part of their responsible computing challenge.
