PhD in Learning Sciences is Live

November 19, 2024 News
phd learning sciences research funding

We are extremely excited to announce that the PhD in Learning Sciences has launched and is accepting applicants. Doc students will prepare for academic and research positions, working closely with MIXI faculty.

Highlights of the program include:

  • full funding with a stipend for highly qualified full time students
  • hybrid coursework based out of Adelphi’s New York City graduate campus
  • opportunities to collaborate and publish with MIXI researches
