Maps @ MIXI: Presents an Evening of Critical Cartography and Spatial Justice

Maps @ MIXI presents 2 artists, researchers, and activists exploring issues of spatial justice in NYC. Artist Alex Wolfe will share his work from walking and photographing the route of the the proposed Interborough Express. Housing expert and preservationist Adam Brodheim discusses his use of open data streams to examine loss of housing due to changes in housing density. The event is free and open to the public.
