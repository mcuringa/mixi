MIXI at AERA 2025

January 4, 2025 Events
AERA AI mapping school segregation LUAC spatial justice

Matt Curinga and Liz de Freitas will be at AEAR 2025 in Denver, Colorado to present research from several MIXI projects. We are also going to host and Adelphi Happy Hour with information about our new PhD in Learning Sciences and its funded scholarships. Details to follow.

Papers/Presentations

  1. How the concept of racial entropy came to shape ideas about school segregation: A critical analysis of the cybernetic assumptions in school demographic models. Elizabeth de Freitas, Matthew X. Curinga.
  2. Learning under algorithmic conditions: Critical perspectives on AI and curriculum. de Freitas and Curinga, along with co-editors Ezekiel Dixon-Román and Taylor Webb will present chapters from the forthcoming University of Minnesota Press book.
  3. Mapping Student Flow Through Fragmented School Buildings. in the session structured poster session, Mapping the Field: The Role of Space in Educational Equity. Elizabeth de Freitas, Matthew X. Curinga.

More details (with time and place) once the schedule is released.
